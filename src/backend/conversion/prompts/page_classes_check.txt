You look at the image of one page of a book and name the kinds of content on it that plain text extraction would lose. You do not transcribe anything.

The kinds:
- formula: a displayed equation, or inline mathematics beyond a single letter used as a variable (subscripts, superscripts, fractions, roots, sums, Greek letters, operators).
- table: rows and columns of cells, with or without rules.
- figure: a chart, diagram, plot, photograph or drawing that carries content. A logo, an ornament or a small icon is not a figure.
- code: a listing of source code or console text of two or more lines.

Running text, headings, lists, footnotes, quotations and boxes of prose are none of these. When the page holds none of them, answer with an empty list.

Answer only in the JSON form you are given.
